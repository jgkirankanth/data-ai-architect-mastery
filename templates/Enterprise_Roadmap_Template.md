# Enterprise Data Architecture Roadmap (FY26 - FY29)

## 1. Strategic Vision & Principles
*   **Vision:** Single source of truth, real-time interoperability, privacy-by-design.
*   **EA Principles:** Cloud-first, API-led integration, Decoupled compute and storage.

## 2. Transition Architectures
### Horizon 1 (Months 0-12): Foundation & Migration
*   Establish Landing Zone and Microsoft Purview governance.
*   Migrate core reporting data to ADLS Gen2 / Lakehouse.

### Horizon 2 (Months 13-24): Interoperability & MDM
*   Implement Canonical Data Models & MDM engine.

### Horizon 3 (Months 25-36): Advanced AI & Self-Service
*   Scale Fabric Workspaces by business domain (Data Mesh approach).
